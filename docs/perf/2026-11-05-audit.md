## Findings

All source paths in this section are relative to src/GymTracker.Web unless prefixed tools/. Production incidence is unknown unless explicitly measured. Reproduced faults demonstrate a reachable failure, not how often a real phone encounters it.

### F1 — A stuck set request blocks every replay trigger

- Journey: logging a set on bad gym signal. One unresolved request holds the queue's single-flight guard; subsequent sets wait even after online/foreground/retry events. Pending data remains stored, so this is stalled delivery rather than proven data loss.
- Evidence: tools/perf/results/client-probes.json; unchanged wwwroot/js/session.js:112–128,150–160,422–440. Injected never-settling fetch plus four simulated 15-second callbacks and online event produces one request, no AbortSignal, one retained pending set. Callback simulation is not an actual 60-second/iPhone timeout measurement.
- Cause: verified missing application send deadline; browser/network settling time unmeasured.
- Severity: user-visible reliability, medium/high for the primary gym journey; affects each blackholed send until browser recovery/reload.

Plan: P1.

### F2 — Retrying one food confirmation logs it twice

- Journey: text food confirmation, noticed as duplicate food/calories after resubmission or a lost response.
- Evidence: duplicate-confirm.json, five independent local parse runs, each exact confirmation POST sent twice: both 302, rows 0→1→2 in every trial. Controllers/FoodController.cs:109–116 always inserts; Features/Feedback/ParseRunService.cs:116–119 returns the same run ID for an already-confirmed run.
- Cause: verified non-idempotent insert path; confirmed-run metadata does not guard the food write. Frequency in production unknown; not alleging existing logs contain duplicates.
- Severity: user-visible data correctness, high on retries.

Plan: P6.

### F3 — Camera can start after closing/leaving its sheet

- Journey: barcode scan while permission/device startup is pending. A late getUserMedia completion can start video/polling after the owner page was torn down.
- Evidence: client-probes.json resolves permission after sheet close AND module cleanup: zero track stops, one video.play, one scanner interval. wwwroot/js/scanner.js:178–213,226–229,252–256; real cleanup contract in wwwroot/js/pages.js:29–37 and wwwroot/js/motion.js:330.
- Cause: verified missing lifecycle invalidation after await. No real camera was opened; native permission dialogs may prevent the exact tap sequence, so iOS incidence/lifetime needs a device check.
- Severity: user-visible/resource risk, medium, conditional on late completion.

Plan: P3.

### F4 — An older navigation response can replace the latest chosen tab

- Journey: rapid tab changes on slow signal. Train requested then Food; Food responds first; late Train overwrites Food.
- Evidence: deterministic shipped-code VM probe in client-probes.json; wwwroot/js/nav.js:254 onward has no request generation/abort ownership check. Browser corroboration and its exact repetitions are recorded in the browser section above.
- Cause: verified response-order race; real-life incidence unmeasured.
- Severity: user-visible reliability, medium, conditional on overlapping responses.

Plan: P4.

### F5 — Food search and parse repeatedly scan the full catalog

- Journey: typing/searching food and text parse. All accounts share a catalog large enough for this cost already.
- Evidence: local 730d search p95 97.1 ms c1/1,016.0 ms c5; fake parse 574.6 ms/6,298.1 ms. Five representative EXPLAINs: median 76.976 ms, Seq Scan 11,795 foods, 293 heap hits/no reads. Features/Food/FoodItemService.cs:36–46,79–97 filters GREATEST(word_similarity(...)) rather than indexable trigram predicates. Features/Food/FoodMatcher.cs:84–105,213–225 builds ranking+shortlists per item, even before matcher-off/own/usual bypass.
- Cause: verified repeated non-index-filtered work; ranking semantics must be preserved. Some up-to-four-search patterns are source-derived for differing EN/BG names, not every local item.
- Severity: user-visible/resource, medium/high, paid on every relevant search/item; local contention becomes visible at c5.

Plan: P5, P7.

### F6 — Restoring a workout does four history commands per exercise

- Journey: reopening the active workout, including after a phone lock.
- Evidence: six-exercise workout consistently 29 queries; local p95 17.2 ms c1/39.9 ms c5. Features/Analytics/TrainingAnalytics.cs:21–35 and 120–127 each loop exercises; RecentSessionSetsAsync: 193–207 issues two commands per call. 4N history commands, 24 of 29 for six exercises. Indexed recent-ID query median 0.021 ms; query count is the issue, not a slow historical scan.
- Cause: verified duplicated suggestions/Last time history reads. Actual users' current production workouts are too sparse to establish frequency.
- Severity: resource and potential phone latency, medium/low after the correctness fixes.

Plan: P8.

### F7 — Model-catalog latency lies outside advertised task deadlines

- Journey: parser, namer, onboarding, weekly and label tasks when the one-hour catalog expires or startup occurs during catalog slowness.
- Evidence: Integrations/OpenRouterFoodParser.cs:99–103 and Integrations/OpenRouterLabelReader.cs:86–90 await body/catalog before attempt cancellation starts; Integrations/OpenRouterFoodNamer.cs:69–76 passes caller ct into body construction despite its 5 s CTS. Program.cs:126–129 gives catalog 10 s timeout; Integrations/OpenRouterModels.cs:39–61 returns last-good on failed refresh without short failure backoff.
- Cause: verified control flow can repeatedly add catalog delay outside task deadline; 10 s is configured worst-case overhead, not measured outage latency. Production parser/matcher/label medians 3,267/1,427.5/4,262 ms establish that seconds matter, not that catalog caused those samples.
- Severity: user-visible latency/failure risk, medium, conditional on cold/expired catalog outage.
- SPEC divergence to resolve before later implementation: weekly writer 20 s attempts versus SPEC 6.5's reference to parser/setup 15 s; label retries HttpRequestException while SPEC 6.6 says 5xx-only. This audit chooses neither side and changes no behavior.

Plan: P11.

### F8 — Nightly restore validation can report false success

- Area: disaster recovery, noticed only when restoration is needed.
- Evidence: tools/restore-drill.sh:25–33 accepts any row/table-manifest difference in nightly mode. tools/perf/restore-drill-probe.py ran the unchanged script with a Docker fixture missing the entire FoodLog table: exit 0, “Restore OK” (2026-11-05-restore-drill.json).
- Cause: verified validator defect; pg_restore --exit-on-error still detects archive/restore command failures. No real production backup was restored or shown corrupt.
- Severity: recovery risk, high confidence/high consequence, low effort.

Plan: P2.

### F9 — First launch and worker replacement need explicit cache budgets

- Journey: first installed-PWA launch and first launch after deploy.
- Evidence: shipped precache 35 assets, 2,005,839 decoded bytes; WASM 1,093,289 (54.5%), Chart.js 208,522, 512 px icon 207,111. wwwroot/sw.js:6–52 uses whole-cache addAll and replacement; layout uses hashed URLs while precache paths are bare (wwwroot/sw.js:72–85, Views/Shared/_Layout.cshtml:51–65). Exact browser initial/update requests, bytes and times are in the browser baseline above.
- Additional measured impact: first Progress waits for two scripts and is 496 ms/1,065 ms slower than warm content in 100/400 ms profiles, with 139–163 ms long tasks.
- Cause: verified full cache population/check strategy. Unchanged resources can use browser HTTP validation; **2 MB of cache content does not mean 2 MB transferred on every deploy**.
- Severity: user-visible launch/update/resource cost, ranked only against measured browser results.

Plan: P12.

### F10 — End-to-end AI and recent error budgets lack durable measurements

- Area: diagnosing phone stalls and failures across deployments.
- Evidence: Features/Feedback/ParseRunService.cs:31–44,59–60 measures parser/matcher phases, awaits naming outside both timed phases, then persists their durations; retained container logs after November 4 contain zero namer/Jev samples. Features/Feedback/ErrorRecorder.cs:37–46 increments lifetime fingerprint Count; Features/Feedback/FeedbackAdmin.cs:227–241 filters LastSeenAt. Exact 30-day occurrence counts cannot be reconstructed.
- Cause: verified incomplete/different retention of stage and occurrence data. Current 30-day active error aggregate is empty; this is an observability gap, not proof of hidden incidents.
- Severity: operational risk/resource, medium.

Plan: P9, P10.
